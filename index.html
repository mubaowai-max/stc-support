How to play

Before every match, pick who you think will win. That pick is required. The other three questions are optional: the final score, the number of yellow cards, and whether there will be a penalty.

Pick the winner only and you can earn 3 points. Answer everything and you can reach 7. A question you skip doesn't earn or cost you anything.

You can change your prediction as often as you like. Predictions lock 5 minutes before kick-off, and every match shows how long is left.

Points per match

3For the correct winner. Calling a draw correctly counts the same as calling a win.
1 + 1One point for each correct number in the score. Predict 2-1 and it ends 2-1, you get 2 points. These count even if your winner pick was wrong.
1For the exact number of yellow cards, from 0 to 5. The 5+ option wins if there are five or more.
1For calling the penalty right: you said there would be one and there was, or you said there wouldn't and there wasn't.

The most you can earn from a single match is 7 points.

Semi-finals and final

There are no draws in the knockout rounds. Pick the team that goes through. If the match goes to a penalty shoot-out, the team that qualifies is the one that counts.

Knockout matches open for predictions once the admin confirms which teams are playing.

Leaderboard

Points are added once the admin confirms each match result. If two players are level on points, the one with more correct winner picks ranks higher.

Other players' names and picks stay hidden until kick-off. Before that, you only see how the votes are split.
